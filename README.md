# Album - 88 The Nations Are His

Seven-song intercession under Jesus Christ as Lord of the nations. Official SCP titles from 5300–6000 named beside prayer overlays only. Stone, Name, Tree, Mount, Sea, Shore, Ice. No living person is named as sovereign, warden, or arbiter. Savior as a brand is refused. PROSAFE is not KJC-DNN. Not affiliated with the SCP Wiki.
